I'm Patrick - a web developer and musician. For work, I build field station research tools for California Digital Library. I'm particularly interested in developer tooling and DX. 

I make music as well - my most recent project is made entirely from vinyl samples. I spend too much money on samplers and records.

## Now
- I'm working on [FAIR Station](https://fairstation.org)
- I'm building practical web tools for musicians, starting with [PrintLyrics](https://printlyrics.app)
- I'm taking guitar lessons again! Ask me about my pedalboard.
- I'm still sitting on my latest musical endeavor, The Radio Show - a sample odyssey about life, death, sex, love, resurrection, grief, and so much more. I love talking about sampling - ethics, legality, and spirituality.
